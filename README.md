# Privacy Policy for FileReady

**Last Updated: May 29, 2026**

## Overview

FileReady is a PDF creation and document scanning app designed with your privacy as the foundation. All document processing — from importing photos to generating PDFs — happens entirely on your device. Nothing leaves your phone without your explicit action.

In short: **What happens on your device, stays on your device.** 🔒

## App Permissions

### Photo Library
FileReady requests access to your photo library **only** when you choose to import photos. Photos are loaded directly into the app for PDF creation. The app never reads, modifies, or uploads your photo library without your explicit action.

### Camera
FileReady requests camera access **only** when you choose to scan a document using the built-in document scanner. Camera images are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

You can manage or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

## Data Collection and Storage

FileReady does **not** collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

- Photos, scans, or images you import
- PDF files you create, compress, or export
- Document content or file names
- Personal information from your documents (names, addresses, ID numbers, etc.)
- Your device's photo library contents

## On-Device Processing

All document processing — including photo import, document scanning, page reordering, rotation, cropping, filtering, PDF generation, and file compression — happens entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server for processing.

## No Account Required

FileReady does not require you to create an account or sign in. You can use all features of the app without providing any personal information.

## Local Storage

All document data (pages, drafts, recent files) is stored locally in the app's sandboxed container on your device. This data is never accessible to other apps or services.

## No Tracking

FileReady does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your usage, collect crash logs automatically, or profile your behavior.

## Purchase Information

If you choose to purchase export credits or subscribe to a Pro plan, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. FileReady does not receive or store your payment information.

## Data Retention

You can delete all app data at any time by removing the app from your device. No residual data remains on any server because no data was ever uploaded.
