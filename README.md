# Privacy Policy for FileReady

**Last Updated:** May 29, 2026

## Overview
Your privacy is our priority. **FileReady** ("the App") is designed to be 100% offline and private. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data.

**In short: What happens on your iPhone, stays on your iPhone.**

## Data Collection and Storage

### On-Device Processing
All document processing — including photo import, document scanning, page reordering, rotation, cropping, filtering, PDF generation, and file compression — happens entirely on your device. No data is sent to any server.

### Local Storage
All document data (pages, drafts, recent files) is stored locally in the app's sandboxed container on your device using Apple's file system. We (the developer) have no access to this data.

## No Tracking
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any third-party analytics services.
- **No Advertising:** The App contains no advertisements and shares no data with ad networks.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account or sign in to use the App.

## Purchase Information
If you choose to purchase export credits or subscribe to a Pro plan, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

## Data Retention
Since we do not host any servers, we do not retain your data. Your data exists only on your device. If you delete the App, your local data will be deleted immediately.
